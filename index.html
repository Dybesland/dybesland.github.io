Sara Dybesland

PhD Researcher in Political Science
European University Institute, Florence

I study political representation, political parties and gender. My research asks whether the issues that matter most to women and men are equally reflected in political agendas, from parties' election manifestos to debates in parliament. I combine large-scale text and survey data with experimental methods, with a particular focus on Norway and Western Europe.
